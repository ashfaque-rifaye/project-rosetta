TV time

Lock the other apps on Fire TV

Rosetta unlocks TV time inside Rosetta and opens Prime Video, YouTube or Netflix when it's earned. Fire TV doesn't let any app lock the others, so use Fire TV's own controls for that:

  1. Settings → Preferences → Parental Controls → turn them On and set a PIN.
  2. Turn on PIN protect app launches, so other apps need your PIN.
  3. Or make an Amazon Kids profile and add only the apps you allow.
  4. Pin Rosetta to the first spot on the home row so it's what the kids see first.
